Support

Questions, bug reports, and feature requests are welcome.

Email: humorwu@gmail.com

When reporting a bug, please include the app version, Windows version, what you did, and what happened. A screenshot helps.

System requirements

  • Windows 10 version 1903 (build 18362) or later, or Windows 11
  • x64 or ARM64
  • Microsoft Edge WebView2 Evergreen Runtime (included with Windows 11; Windows 10 may need it)

Where are my settings stored?

Settings, sessions, and prediction data stay on your device in the app's MSIX package container. They are not uploaded and are removed when you uninstall the app.

How do I uninstall?

Open Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find Caelum Files, and choose Uninstall. You can also right-click the app in the Start menu.

Does Caelum Files collect data?

No. There is no telemetry, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or account. See the privacy policy.

我的设置保存在哪里？

设置、会话和预测数据只保存在本机的 MSIX 应用包容器中，不会上传，卸载应用后会被删除。

如何卸载？

打开 设置 → 应用 → 已安装的应用，找到 Caelum Files，选择卸载；也可以在开始菜单中右键应用卸载。

Caelum Files 会收集数据吗？

不会。应用没有遥测、统计分析、广告、崩溃上报或账号。详见隐私政策。
