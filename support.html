When reporting a bug, please include the app version, macOS version, what you did, and what happened. A screenshot helps.

System requirements

  • macOS 15 or later
  • Apple Silicon or Intel Mac

A folder will not open

The App Store edition can only access folders you grant. Open Settings → Permissions → File Access, click Add Folder…, and choose the folder or one of its parent folders.

The direct edition can access the file system, but protected locations such as Desktop, Documents, or external volumes may need Full Disk Access in System Settings → Privacy & Security.

Terminal does not open

Allow Caelum Files to control your terminal in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation. You can also choose a different terminal in Settings → Terminal.

Archive cannot be extracted

Caelum Files supports ZIP, TAR, TAR.GZ, and GZ. Encrypted ZIP, RAR, 7Z, bzip2, and zstd archives are not supported. Absolute paths, .., and symlink escapes are rejected by design.

How do I change the language?

Caelum Files follows the system language. On macOS 13 or later, you can choose a language for Caelum Files only in System Settings → Language & Region → Applications → Caelum Files.

Where are my settings and session stored?

In the app's sandbox container on the App Store edition, or in the standard preferences folder for the direct edition. Removing the app or its container resets them.

Does Caelum Files collect data?

No. There is no telemetry, analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or account. See the privacy policy.

系统要求

  • macOS 15 或更高
  • Apple Silicon 或 Intel Mac

文件夹打不开

App Store 版只能访问你授权的文件夹。打开设置 → 权限 → 文件访问，点添加文件夹…，选择该文件夹或其上级文件夹。

直装版可以访问文件系统，但桌面、文稿、外置宗卷等受保护位置可能需要在系统设置 → 隐私与安全性中开启完全磁盘访问。

终端没有打开

在系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 自动化中允许 Caelum Files 控制终端。也可以在设置 → 终端中换一个终端程序。

归档无法解压

Caelum Files 支持 ZIP、TAR、TAR.GZ 和 GZ。加密 ZIP、RAR、7Z、bzip2、zstd 不受支持。绝对路径、..、符号链接逃逸等不安全路径会被拒绝。

如何切换语言？

Caelum Files 跟随系统语言。macOS 13 及以上可以在系统设置 → 语言与地区 → 应用程序 → Caelum Files中单独选择语言。

设置和会话保存在哪里？

App Store 版保存在应用的沙盒容器；直装版保存在标准 preferences 目录。删除应用或容器会重置这些数据。

Caelum Files 会收集数据吗？

不会。应用没有遥测、统计分析、广告、崩溃上报或账号。详见隐私政策。
